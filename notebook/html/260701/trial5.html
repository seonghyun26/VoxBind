VoxBind · autoresearch · dramatic change #5

Trial 5 — Finer patches (1 Å resolution)

architecturespatial resolution base = ChannelViT [7,4,2] (ρ≈0.637) · done — ρ 0.639 (tie, +0.002)

Hypothesis

The whole campaign used patch_size=8 (2 Å patches). Maybe the frozen-probe plateau is partly a spatial-resolution limit — the encoder reads density gradients / pocket detail only at 2 Å. Drop to patch_size=4 (1 Å patches) so it sees structure at 2× finer resolution: 16³=4096 patches/group → 12,288 ChannelViT tokens (8× the token count). No new code — a pure architectural knob.

Experiment & result

exp260629_ar_cvit_g742_patch4 · model.patch_size=4, bsz2×accum16 (eff-128), compile OFF
cost12,288 tokens/forward → ~1300 s/epoch, ~34 h for 100 ep (8× the base)
resultρ 0.639 ±.002 · r 0.656 · RMSE 1.432  →  +0.002 vs 0.637 (tie), worse RMSE (3 seeds)

Finer resolution does not help. 8× the tokens and 34 h of compute buy a dead tie on ρ (within the ±0.02 seed floor) and a slightly worse RMSE (1.432 vs 1.353). Spatial resolution is not the bottleneck — the 2 Å patches already capture what the affinity probe can use. Folded into Phase 8.
