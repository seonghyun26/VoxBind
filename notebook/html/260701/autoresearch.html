Reading it

Density lifts ChannelViT over coords (best 0.641 vs coords-ChannelViT 0.604). The winning structure is group density+gradmag together, keep lig/poc separate ([7,4,2] > [11,2] > [7,4,1,1] > [11,1,1]).

The knob space is essentially flat. Across every trial, only grouping ever moved ρ. atom-bias (mask chemistry-rich voxels) is the lone nominal beat at ρ 0.641, but its ±.006 overlaps the [7,4,2] base (0.637) and its RMSE is worse (1.385 vs 1.353) — better ranking, worse calibration, i.e. within noise. Mask ratio (0.6/0.75), recon weight (0.3 tied, 1.0 worse), rope3d (0.610), wd0.1 (0.624) and HCS channel-group dropout (0.637/0.633) all tie-or-hurt.

Capacity is NOT the bottleneck. Doubling the encoder (dim768, 97.9M, 2.1×) regressed to ρ 0.626 (−0.011, worst RMSE) — a bigger model just overfits the frozen probe. So the plateau is data scale / probe-ceiling bound, not model size or pretext recipe. The masking-distribution axis is now closed too (mask 0.4 / block 4 hurt).
