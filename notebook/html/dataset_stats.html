PDBbind Density Normalisation — Dataset Statistics

Five normalisation variants of the experimental 2Fo-Fc pocket density crops · 64³ @ 0.25 Å · 4,827 data points (complexes; all five versions = 4,827 crops each). Distribution plots below are computed over a random 200-crop sample = 52,428,800 voxels per version.
v1 · per-map z + per-crop ±3σ clipv2 · pocket-pool z-scorev3 · pocket-pool max-absv4 · pocket-pool clip + z-scorev5 · pocket-pool arcsinh + z-score
Key point. v1 is the only per-crop scheme — it discards cross-pocket density magnitude and clips peaks. v2–v5 are pool-global; v2 and v3 are the same linear map up to a global ×0.0109 scalar (v3 is just v2 shrunk ~92×), v4 hard-clips the tail then z-scores, and v5 instead soft-squashes it with arcsinh (no clip → no pile-up spike). Because the MAE reconstructs the density channel with plain MSE and the on-disk scale is fed unchanged, each scheme's scale silently sets how heavily density is weighted in the loss (∝ scale²) — which drives the downstream ranking below.

5 · Why it matters for training

The density channel is reconstructed by the MAE pretext with plain per-element MSE and no per-channel renormalisation (compute_losses), and the crop is fed as-is (normalize:false, sigma_noise:0). So a scheme's value-scale ∝ its reconstruction-loss weight. v2 = "density loud" (unit scale), v3 = "density muted" (~10⁴× smaller). The frozen-encoder PDBbind affinity probe (Spearman ρ, merged_density encoder) is monotonic in that loudness:

Forcing experimental density into the representation (v2, dual-head v4) degrades affinity here.
