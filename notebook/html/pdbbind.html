LP-PDBBind Data Quality Filters (CL columns)

CL columns are data quality filters, not similarity-leakage flags. True = passes the filter (higher quality); False = excluded at that level. The three filters are cumulative (CL3 ⊆ CL2 ⊆ CL1):
• CL1: QED > 0.2 · only common elements (≥ 1‰) · protein–ligand heavy-atom distance 1.75–4 Å (no clashes) — applies to all categories
• CL2: CL1 + binding affinity is exact Kd or Ki (IC50 excluded) — applies to all categories
• CL3: CL1 + CL2 + entry is in the refined or core subset — general entries are always CL3=False by definition

Our EDS-filtered subset (refined category, EDS available) spans all CL levels — we do not apply the CL quality filters.

Note on Timesplit coverage: The paper's original timesplit has 363 test / 968 val entries. Our version has 304 / 820 because 3,181 entries in the combined index failed to parse during LMDB processing (missing or broken structure files). The missing 59 test entries may bias our test metrics upward — results should not be directly compared to the paper's Table 9 numbers without this caveat.

Train–Test Nearest-Neighbour Similarity

Per-test-entry max similarity to any training entry. Protein: SequenceMatcher ratio (proxy for Needleman-Wunsch identity). Ligand: Dice on Morgan fingerprints (radius=2, 2048 bits). Protein train sample = 1,000 (random). A pair is flagged if protein > 0.50 and ligand > 0.99 simultaneously.

LP-PDBBind's explicit Needleman-Wunsch + Dice filtering eliminates all leaky pairs. The timesplit (temporal cut only) leaves 22 test entries with a close training neighbor above both thresholds. Higher median protein similarity in the timesplit (0.60 vs 0.34) reflects the absence of sequence-based deduplication.
