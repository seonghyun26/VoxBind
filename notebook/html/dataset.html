Density annotation — gathered corpus Summary

The trustworthy density-matched data, pooled across sources: every entry carries an experimental electron-density crop whose signal corresponds to both the pocket and the modeled ligand. Two sources — CrossDocked2020 (ligand-matched v6 subset) and PDBbind / LP-PDBBind (real holo crystals, PDBe-EDS). Per-source detail in the sections below.

⚠ Does density exist for cross-docked samples? Yes — but it is the receptor's density, not the docked ligand's.

Each entry is named {receptor}_{chain}_rec_{ligand-PDB}_{resname}_lig_tt_{docked|min}_{rank}, e.g. 4xe6_X_rec_3fqc_55v_lig_tt_docked_4 = ligand 55v taken from PDB 3fqc and computationally docked into receptor 4xe6.
  • The CCP4 map is downloaded and the crop is validated by the receptor PDB id only (here 4xe6) — alignment fits the pocket atoms to the deposited receptor structure and drops a crop on weak density at the receptor atoms. The ligand identity is never consulted.
  • So for the 86% cross-docked majority, the electron density in the ligand region reflects whatever 4xe6 actually crystallized (its own native ligand / solvent), not the docked 55v pose. The pocket / protein density is faithful for every sample; the ligand-region density only matches the modeled ligand for the native self-poses (~7–14%).
  • Practically: the density channel is best read as a faithful receptor-context signal, available for ~79% of all pairs regardless of native vs cross-docked — not as ground-truth density for the generated ligand.

✔ Density v6 — the ligand-matched subset (fixes the mismatch above)

v6 keeps a crop only where the experimental density corresponds to both the pocket and the modeled ligand: available_v6 = density_available & is_native.
  • Pocket match (inherited from v5): Kabsch-aligned pocket atoms + real density at the receptor atoms.
  • Ligand match (new --native_filter tt_min): the modeled ligand is the receptor's own crystal ligand in its crystal pose (tt_min, ligand PDB = receptor PDB) — so the ligand-region density is genuinely that ligand's, not a cross-docked stand-in.
  • Same v5 arcsinh+z normalization (joint train+test pool) → directly comparable to v5; v5 is left untouched.

✔ Density v7 — combined matched corpus (CrossDocked v6 ∪ PDBbind-2020-train)

v7 unions the v6 ligand-matched CrossDocked crops with the PDBbind-2020 train split — real holo crystals where density inherently matches pocket + ligand — into one CrossDocked-style pretraining corpus (data_train_v7.pt + xray_crops_aligned_v7/). ~1.5× the v6 matched data.
