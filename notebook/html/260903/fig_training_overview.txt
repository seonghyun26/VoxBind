============================================================
Goal: a single wide, left-to-right schematic ("Figure 1a: Method overview" style,
as in BioEmu-CV / TPS-DPS) that shows how we pre-train the density-aware voxel
encoder with a masked-autoencoding objective, and how the frozen encoder transfers
to affinity prediction. Clean, flat, publication-quality vector look (think
matplotlib/TikZ, not 3D render). White background, thin arrows, muted scientific
palette. Roughly 5 stages arranged horizontally with labeled arrows between them.

STAGE 1 — Input complex.
Left-most: a small cartoon of a protein--ligand complex (pocket as a light gray
surface/ribbon, ligand as sticks in a distinct color) sitting inside a translucent
cube that marks the 64^3 voxel crop centered on the pocket. Label: "protein--ligand
complex". A short arrow "voxelize" points right.

STAGE 2 — Multi-channel voxel grid (13 channels).
Show the crop expanded into a stack of 3D grids, visually grouped into FOUR blocks
with a small brace/label under each group:
  - "ligand atoms (7)"  — 7 thin translucent voxel cubes, warm color.
  - "pocket atoms (4)"  — 4 thin translucent voxel cubes, cool color.
  - "electron density rho (1)" — 1 grid rendered as a smooth cloud/isosurface, teal.
  - "gradient |grad rho| (1)"  — 1 grid rendered as an edge/shell pattern, purple.
Caption under the whole stack: "x in R^{13 x 64^3}  =  [ atoms | density | gradient ]".
Emphasize that density + gradient are the extra channels vs. a coords-only encoder
(e.g. a small "+density" badge on the last two grids).

STAGE 3 — Atom-biased 3D block masking.
Take the 13-channel stack and show the SAME spatial mask applied across channels:
partition into 8^3 blocks, ~60% masked (drawn as darkened/hollow cubes), the rest
visible. Draw the masked blocks concentrated around the ligand/interface region (NOT
uniformly), with a small heat overlay on atom density to convey "atom-biased: mask
where the atoms are". Label: "mask 60% of 8^3 blocks (atom-biased)". Arrow "mask" ->.

STAGE 4 — ChannelViT encoder + reconstruction head (the MAE core).
Center-piece. Visible (unmasked) voxel patches flow into a box labeled
"channel-grouped 3D ViT (ChannelViT) f_theta  (~45M, patch 8^3, dim 512, depth 12)".
Key detail: show the FOUR channel groups (ligand atoms / pocket atoms / density /
gradient) tokenized SEPARATELY -> four parallel token streams (color-coded per group)
that merge into one joint self-attention stack, conveying per-group tokens + cross-group
attention rather than a single fused patch embedding. From the encoder,
a thin "lightweight per-patch head h_psi" box outputs a reconstructed 13-channel grid
"x_hat". Place the reconstruction directly above/below the ORIGINAL clean grid
"x (target)" so the reader sees prediction vs. target only at masked voxels.
Draw a dashed "EMA (0.999)" copy of the encoder beside f_theta with a small arrow,
tagged "exported weights".

STAGE 5 — Loss.
Between x_hat and x, a small callout box with the objective:
"L = masked-voxel MSE,  weighted:  inv-freq per atom-type (clip 30x),
 atom-on voxels 10x,  density/gradient 0.1x". Arrow labels "reconstruct masked voxels".

TRANSFER (bottom strip, visually separated by a light divider + label "Downstream tasks").
Show the SAME encoder box, now tagged "f_theta (EMA, pre-trained)", feeding TWO parallel
downstream branches. Draw both branches stacked and smaller than the pre-training row so they

  (1) Binding affinity — put a snowflake "frozen" icon on the encoder; its patch tokens ->

      ligand placed in the pocket. Label the connecting arrow "condition".
