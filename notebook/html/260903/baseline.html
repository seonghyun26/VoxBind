Five structure-based generative baselines, one evaluation path

•The baselines

All five are pocket-conditioned generative models trained on the same CrossDocked2020 split and run here from the checkpoints their authors released. They differ mainly in whether the ligand is grown atom by atom, denoised as a whole, or represented as a field.

  • AR Luo et al., NeurIPS 2021
    Autoregressive: places one atom at a time into the pocket, choosing each position from a learned 3D density over the empty space.
  • Pocket2Mol Peng et al., ICML 2022
    Also autoregressive, but predicts a focal atom and then samples the next atom and its bonds from an E(3)-equivariant network, so bonds come out of the model rather than a post-hoc perception step.
  • DiffSBDD Schneuing et al., Nat Comput Sci 2024
    Equivariant diffusion over atom positions and types conditioned on the full-atom pocket; the whole ligand is denoised at once rather than grown.
  • DecompDiff Guan et al., ICML 2023
    Diffusion with decomposed priors — arms and scaffold get separate priors placed in the pocket. Run here with the reference-ligand prior.
  • FuncBind Kirchmeyer et al., arXiv 2025
    Represents the molecule as a continuous neural field rather than a fixed atom set, so one model spans drug modalities; scored here on its small-molecule CrossDocked samples.
  • Reference CrossDocked2020
    The crystal ligand of each test pocket, scored through the identical pipeline — the control that says whether the evaluation path itself is right.

1Vina evaluation

TargetDiff's evaluate_from_meta.py, unmodified apart from an empty-pocket guard and a thread-count passthrough. AutoDock Vina 1.2.2, exhaustiveness 32, docking into the full receptor (*_rec.pdb, ~2,100 atoms) with the search box centred on the generated ligand plus a 5 Å buffer.

Pipeline check Scoring the 100 reference ligands reproduces the TargetDiff paper exactly — Vina Score −6.36, Min −6.71, Dock −7.45, QED 0.476, SA 0.728. Pocket assignment was also checked physically: for every baseline the generated molecules sit 0.2–7.7 Å from the centroid of that pocket's reference ligand, none beyond 15 Å.
  • Vina Score — binding energy of the generated pose exactly as the model produced it, no relaxation.
  • Vina Min — the same pose after local energy minimisation, without re-searching the pocket.
  • Vina Dock — best energy after a full re-dock into the pocket.
  • High affinity — share of generated molecules docking at least as well as that pocket's reference ligand.
  • QED — drug-likeness, 0 to 1, higher is better.  SA — synthetic accessibility rescaled to 0–1, higher is easier to make.
  • Diversity — 1 minus the mean pairwise RDKFingerprint Tanimoto within a pocket.
  • Success — share of molecules clearing QED > 0.25, SA > 0.59 and Vina Dock < −8.18 together.

1cWhat the success rate is a share of

Success rate and high affinity are shares of the molecules that reached the Vina pass. Molecules a model fails to build are dropped before that point — targetdiff's evaluation skips any SMILES containing a . — so they cost nothing, and a model that discards more of its own output is scored on the part of it that worked. That is the field's convention, and it is invisible unless the denominator is written down.

Only DiffSBDD, DecompDiff and FuncBind take a fixed 100 shots per pocket, so only their per-draw figures are exact. AR and Pocket2Mol keep sampling until they have enough and write only what passes their own filters (10,862 and 11,970 molecules, 36–258 per pocket), so their rejected attempts are never recorded and their per-draw numbers are upper bounds. DecompDiff is the one baseline where the choice of denominator changes the ranking: of its 10,000 draws, 8,774 rebuilt as RDKit molecules and 8,207 came out as a single connected fragment, which moves its success rate from 15.6 % to 12.8 % and its high affinity from 48.8 % to 40.0 %. Its decomposed prior seeds arms and scaffold as separate blobs that the model has to bond together, and the loss tracks the arm count — 87.6 valid molecules per 100 draws at one arm, 63.7 at three.

2PoseCheck evaluation

PoseCheck scores the pose the model produced rather than a re-docked one, so it asks a different question from Vina: is the geometry physically sane before docking software gets a chance to fix it? Receptors are protonated with pdb2pqr and scored against the same full-atom structures used for docking.

  • Clashes — number of steric overlaps between ligand and protein heavy atoms; lower is better.
  • Strain — internal energy of the generated conformer above its relaxed form, kcal/mol. Reported as the median: the distribution has a long tail where UFF relaxation diverges, and a handful of molecules move the mean by orders of magnitude.
  • HB donor / acceptor / hydrophobic / vdW — mean number of each interaction type formed with the pocket, from prolif.

ASetup & porting

The GPUs here are Blackwell (compute capability 12.0). All four repositories pin a PyTorch that predates that architecture and cannot launch a single kernel on it, so every model was moved onto a common torch 2.10 / cu128 stack.

sm_120 GPUs
The pinned torch 1.8–1.13 builds stop at sm_90 and raise “no kernel image is available”. All four now run on torch 2.10.0+cu128 with source-built torch_scatter, torch_cluster and torch_sparse.
NumPy 2.x
np.int, np.float and np.bool were removed; AR and DecompDiff used them while parsing ligands, silently skipping every datapoint.
Ragged arrays
Pocket2Mol built np.array over per-candidate log-prob rows of different lengths, which NumPy now refuses — it killed 13 pockets. The ranking function only sums each row, so the rows are passed through as a list.
Empty beam
AR takes a softmax over its beam candidates without checking the beam is non-empty; two pockets exhausted it and crashed. The loop now stops and keeps what it has.
PyG 2.8
torch_geometric.utils.subgraph is no longer a module, and torch_cluster's kernels reject non-contiguous inputs, so Pocket2Mol's knn and radius calls are wrapped.
Biopython 1.86
three_to_one was removed; DiffSBDD's pocket parsing uses a shim over protein_letters_3to1.
torch.load
2.6 flipped weights_only to True, rejecting all of these checkpoints. DiffSBDD's Lightning 1.8 checkpoint is upgraded to 2.6 on read.
Two environments
Sampling needs python 3.12 for the modern CUDA stack, but Vina 1.2.2 has no wheel for it — only 1.2.7. Evaluation therefore stays in the older env that has 1.2.2, and molecules cross between them as SDF, since a meta pickled under numpy 2 cannot be read under numpy 1.24.
Pocket indexing
AR and Pocket2Mol originally indexed 99 pockets: Y635_MYCTU_1_158_0 was dropped while staging their raw data, which also shifted every later data_id. Collection follows each sampler's own pocket order rather than assuming it matches the split, and the missing pocket was sampled separately from a one-entry dataset.
Sampling subset
AR and Pocket2Mol finish small molecules first — across 15 pockets their first 50 average ~14 atoms against ~20 for the next 50. Any subset of a pocket's ligands is therefore taken evenly across generation order, never as a prefix, which moved AR's mean molecule size from 13.7 to 16.8 atoms (published: ~17.7).
Docking in halves
A Vina worker holds a pocket until its last molecule is docked, so the 100 ligands are split into two disjoint strided halves scored in separate passes — verified to have zero overlap and to sum to the full generated set.
PoseCheck adaptations
Its protonation step (hydride) fails here with a Cython dtype error, so receptors go through pdb2pqr instead. Metal ions in ~30 receptors need explicit vdW radii for MDAnalysis to guess bonds. And prolif must be called with n_jobs=1: left unset it spawns one process per CPU inside every pool worker.
FuncBind samples
Taken from the run already on disk (2026-08-03) rather than re-sampled: its hydra config records n_targets=100, n_samples_per_receptor=100, split=test and its own docking turned off, and its test order matches this split 100/100. Only the scoring here is ours.
Negative residue ids
Five receptors number residues from a negative index and prolif stores residue numbers as uint32, so PoseCheck failed on those pockets for every model. The numbering is shifted before scoring; only interaction counts are reported, not which residue each one hit.
Not run
DecompDiff's beta-prior variant. Its published priors are one file per pocket on Google Drive and the download stops at 75 of 100 on a quota error; the ref-prior variant needs no such file.

BReproducing

Generation and evaluation are separate. Each baseline writes into _out/, is collected into TargetDiff's meta format, then scored.

# generate — one process per pocket, sharded across GPUs
bash _scripts/run_baseline.sh Pocket2Mol 2,3 13
bash _scripts/run_diffsbdd.sh 4,5 4
bash _scripts/run_decomp.sh ref_prior 6,7 4

# collect, then score with Vina 1.2.2 (half 1; BASELINE_MOLS_PART=2 for the rest)
BASELINE_MAX_MOLS=50 BASELINE_MOLS_PART=1 python _scripts/build_meta.py Pocket2Mol
python _scripts/evaluate_from_meta_mp.py _meta/Pocket2Mol.pt \
    -n 100 --docking_mode vina_full --exhaustiveness 32 --skip_existing True \
    --protein_root targetdiff/data/test_set --result_path _eval/Pocket2Mol

# PoseCheck over the generated poses
python _scripts/run_posecheck.py Pocket2Mol 30 20

# aggregate both and rebuild this page
python _scripts/summarize.py && python _scripts/summarize_posecheck.py
python _scripts/make_report.py
Vina 1.2.2, exhaustiveness 32, full receptor, box = generated ligand extent + 5 Å. Metric definitions follow targetdiff/notebooks/summary.ipynb.
