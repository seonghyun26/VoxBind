Reference-ligand similarity, four ways

The same nine rows of reference_similarity.csv, drawn four ways in the palette and furniture of the Vina charts they will sit beside. Pick one; the other three get deleted along with their branch of the builder.

pockets 79 shared similarity ECFP4 Morgan r=2 / 2048-bit Tanimoto scaffold Bemis-Murcko exact match aggregation per-pocket mean, macro-averaged

What the numbers actually say. Eight of the nine methods sit between 0.089 and 0.118 mean Tanimoto — a band narrow enough that the ranking inside it is not a result. DecompDiff is the exception at 0.152 and 2.17% scaffold reuse, roughly double everyone else on the scaffold axis.

Ours lands at 0.099 / 0.81%: mid-pack on atom similarity, second-lowest scaffold reuse of the nine. The honest reading is “no method is copying the crystal ligand, and ours copies its scaffold less than most” — every variant below is built to support that claim and none of them to inflate it.
