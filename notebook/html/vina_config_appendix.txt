For all VINA-based evaluations, we use AutoDock Vina 1.2 \citep{eberhardt2021autodock}
through its Python bindings, on an identical configuration as follows:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \texttt{version}: 1.2.2
    \item \texttt{sf\_name} (scoring function): \texttt{vina} (the default Vina 1.2
          scoring function; neither \texttt{vinardo} nor \texttt{ad4})
    \item \texttt{exhaustiveness}: 16
    \item \texttt{n\_poses} (\texttt{num\_modes}): 1
    \item \texttt{energy\_range}: 3.0 kcal/mol (Vina default; inactive in practice, as
          only the top-ranked pose is retained)
    \item \texttt{min\_rmsd}: 1.0 \AA{} (Vina default)
    \item \texttt{max\_evals}: 0 (Vina's built-in heuristic)
    \item \texttt{seed}: 0 (Vina draws a random seed for every run)
    \item \texttt{cpu}: 1 thread per Vina instance, with 20 instances run concurrently
    \item \texttt{center}: the centroid of the axis-aligned bounding box of the ligand
          under evaluation, $(\mathbf{x}_{\max}+\mathbf{x}_{\min})/2$; the box therefore
          follows the generated pose for sampled molecules and the crystal pose for the
          reference ligand
    \item \texttt{box\_size}: ligand-adaptive,
          $(\mathbf{x}_{\max}-\mathbf{x}_{\min})+5$\,\AA{} along each axis; across the
          9{,}195 molecules generated for the 92 test pockets this yields a mean box of
          $12.1\times12.0\times11.8$\,\AA$^3$ (median $11.8\times11.5\times11.3$\,\AA$^3$;
          5th--95th percentile of the per-axis edge length 7.5--18.3\,\AA)
    \item \texttt{receptor}: the CrossDocked 10\,\AA{} pocket crop
          (\texttt{*\_pocket10.pdb}), held rigid
    \item \texttt{receptor preparation}: \texttt{pdb2pqr30 --ff=AMBER} (v3.6.1; standard
          protonation states at pH 7.0, no PROPKA titration), followed by AutoDockTools
          \texttt{prepare\_receptor4.py} with its defaults (Gasteiger charges, non-polar
          hydrogens merged, lone pairs / waters / non-standard residues removed, no
          structural repairs)
    \item \texttt{ligand preparation}: RDKit \texttt{AddHs(addCoords=True)}, which
          preserves the generated 3D coordinates (no re-embedding and no conformer
          regeneration), followed by Meeko \texttt{MoleculePreparation()} with its
          defaults (\texttt{merge\_hydrogens=True}, \texttt{hydrate=False}, rigid
          macrocycles, rigid amide bonds); rotatable bonds are detected automatically
    \item \texttt{Vina Score} mode: \texttt{Vina.score()} — a single-point evaluation of
          the generated pose exactly as produced by the model, with no search and no
          relaxation
    \item \texttt{Vina Min} mode: \texttt{Vina.optimize()} with \texttt{max\_steps}=0 —
          local energy minimization initialized from the generated pose, relaxing the
          ligand degrees of freedom against the rigid receptor
    \item \texttt{Vina Dock} mode: \texttt{Vina.dock(exhaustiveness=16, n\_poses=1)} —
          a full Monte-Carlo global search within the box, i.e. the molecule is re-docked
          from scratch, and the affinity of the top-ranked pose is reported
\end{itemize}

The reference ligand of each pocket is processed through the identical three-mode
protocol; the High-Affinity percentage is the fraction of generated molecules whose Vina
Dock score is at least as low as that of the corresponding reference ligand.

[2] 본문에 그대로 쓰면 안 되는 것
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - box는 고정 크기가 아니라 리간드마다 달라짐 → "xxx × xxx × xxx" 단일 수치 불가.
    위처럼 규칙 + 실측 통계로 기술함.
  - energy_range는 우리가 지정한 값이 아니라 Vina.energies()의 기본값이며,
    n_poses=1이라 실제 필터링은 일어나지 않음.
  - seed 고정 안 함 → bitwise 재현성 없음.
  - 큰 리간드에서 Vina가 "Search space volume is greater than 27000 Angstrom^3" 경고를
    냄. 설정상 정상 동작이며 결과는 유효.
