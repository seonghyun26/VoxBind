  • Protocol: 79 CrossDocked test pockets × 100 valid-unique molecules each (7,900 total).
  • Docking: AutoDock Vina — score at the generated pose, min after local minimization, dock after full redocking.
  • High Affinity (HA): fraction of generated molecules scoring below the crystal ligand redocked in the same box.
  • Aggregation: two reductions, in this order. Within a pocket, average its 100 molecules into one value — this is what the pocket scores. Then summarize the 79 pocket values: mean and median are two readings of that same distribution, matching the Avg./Med. convention in results.html. Median sits well below mean because a handful of pockets carry clashing poses with large positive Vina scores. max columns instead take the per-pocket maximum, so a single near-duplicate cannot hide in a mean.
  • Decision rule: arms are ranked by docking, never by training loss — the best-loss arm to date is also the worst-docking one.

Findings.

  • Only Trial 1 beats the baseline. Δ −0.26 on Vina dock mean, −0.73 on median, +2.2 pt HA. Trials 3 and 4 land within ±0.06 of the baseline (a tie), Trials 2 and 5 lose. Neither encoder capacity (45.7M → 64.6M) nor injection depth is the lever.
  • σ = 1.0 erases the density gain. Trial 2 falls to −7.79, level with the σ1.0 density-free run (−7.798).
  • The gain is not reproduction. Trial 1 leads no similarity metric: its Morgan max (0.248), Dice max (0.426) and scaffold-match rate (0.82%) all sit at or below the baseline. Scaffold identity stays under 1.5% everywhere, and five independent fingerprint families agree on the ranking.
  • 3D shape is the one axis where every density arm exceeds the baseline (0.375 → 0.392–0.452). This is not ligand-density leakage: Trial 3, which deletes the ligand from the density map, has the highest overlap of all (0.452). Shape overlap does grow with molecule size, so a heavy-atom-controlled comparison is still needed before this is called an effect.
  • Trial 5 is the least reference-similar and the weakest. Lowest on Morgan, Dice and scaffold match while docking worst (Δ +0.42, HA 59.3% — the only arm below 60%) — under-conditioned rather than novel. It also has the worst SA by a wide margin (0.606 vs 0.67–0.69 everywhere else), so the molecules are harder to make as well as weaker binders. Injecting density inside the UNet is the wrong pathway, at least at this σ and encoder.
  • Caveat: Trials 4–5 trained on 78,512 crops against 99,881 for the baseline and Trials 1–3, so those comparisons move two axes at once. Only baseline ↔ 1 ↔ 2 ↔ 3 is a clean single-axis contrast.
