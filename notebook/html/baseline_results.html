1 Dataset — lp_edrscc_v2

Frozen, version-controlled split (voxbind/splits/lp_edrscc_v2.csv; verify.py PASS, sha256 pinned 55122339…). Defined as LP-PDBBind new_split ∩ ED-available ∩ (ligand & pocket RSCC ≥ 0.8), non-covalent, Kd/Ki only — i.e. lp_edrscc_v1 with all IC50 dropped (4,141 assay-dependent measurements removed). Partition is the LP-PDBBind sequence-identity clustering → novel-target generalization (zero PDB overlap across train/val/test). Label = pK (−log Kd/Ki).

2 EGNN & EGNN + TargetDiff — binding-affinity regression

Two affinity-prediction baselines from the TargetDiff paper (ICLR 2023), trained/evaluated on lp_edrscc_v2 (test n=1,319). EGNN = the plain supervised EGNN trained from scratch (configs/prop/pdbbind_edrscc_v2_egnn.yml; 6-layer, 256 hidden, knn=48, cutoff=10 Å; 2.547 M params). EGNN + TargetDiff = the same head on the pretrained TargetDiff diffusion-encoder features (egnn_enc, enc_feature_type=final_h). Metrics are mean ± std over 3 seeds (0/1/2).
