TargetDiff affinity baselines — LP-EDRSCC v2

📅 2026-06-24 🧪 PDBBind v2020 · lp_edrscc_v2 📊 test n=1,319 · 3 seeds · mean ± std ⚙ voxbind env · RTX PRO 6000
TL;DR. On the Kd/Ki-only, RSCC-filtered lp_edrscc_v2 split, augmenting the supervised EGNN with pretrained TargetDiff diffusion features (final_h) improves binding-affinity regression: test Pearson 0.539 → 0.597 (+0.058), RMSE 1.531→1.447, R² 0.251→0.330 — consistent across Kd and Ki. Both EGNN and EGNN+TargetDiff are final (3 seeds × 200 epochs, best-val selection).

3 · Setup & notes

  • EGNN — paper's supervised EGNN from scratch (configs/prop/pdbbind_edrscc_v2_egnn.yml; 6-layer, 256 hidden, knn=48, cutoff=10 Å; 2.55 M params). 200 epochs, best-val selection. Final.
  • EGNN + TargetDiff — same head on pretrained TargetDiff diffusion features (enc_feature_type=final_h, 128-dim per protein+ligand atom). The diffusion model was trained from scratch on CrossDocked (4-GPU DDP, global batch 32, converged best-val ckpt at iter 106k); final_h was dumped via fetch_embedding for all 5,981 v2 complexes (per-complex atom alignment verified).
  • Status: complete — 3 seeds × 200 epochs; metrics at each seed's best-val checkpoint (epochs 6 / 15 / 9). Best-val plateaued early, so these are the final numbers (unchanged from the in-flight values).
  • Takeaway — TargetDiff diffusion features give a consistent +0.05–0.06 Pearson / lower RMSE over the plain EGNN across All / Kd / Ki, matching the paper's finding that unsupervised generative representations aid supervised affinity prediction.
