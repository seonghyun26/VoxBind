VoxBind Channel-ViT frozen probe — model-size scaling

📅 2026-06-24 🧪 PDBBind v2020 · lp_edrscc_v2 📊 test n=1,320 · 3 seeds · mean ± std ⚙ voxbind env · RTX PRO 6000 (GPU 0/1)
TL;DR. Two PLINDER-pretrained Channel-ViT encoders (C+D+G, OTF, seed 0), frozen and linear-probed for affinity on the full lp_edrscc_v2 split. Scaling the encoder 4.7× (0.4M → 4M tier; 1.19M → 5.63M params) clearly helps: test Spearman 0.615 vs 0.577 (Δ +0.039, ~10× the per-seed σ), Pearson 0.631 vs 0.599, RMSE 1.386 vs 1.440. The 4M probe even edges past the external supervised references on this split (HBGSA 3.06M r=0.565, EGNN+TargetDiff r=0.597). Note the reversal: on the earlier ~50%-coverage subset (test n=646) the two tiers tied (ρ 0.563 vs 0.562) — an artifact of partial local data. Completing the split to 100% (5,986/5,987) restored the scaling signal. Both rows are final (3 seeds, identical test hash).

On the same split, the external supervised references are HBGSA 3.06M r=0.565, plain EGNN r=0.539, EGNN+TargetDiff r=0.597 (see results_hbgsa.html / results_targetdiff.html). The frozen 4M Channel-ViT probe (r=0.631) tops all of them and the 0.4M (r=0.599) matches EGNN+TargetDiff — encouraging for self-supervised density pretraining. The 4M also early-stops sooner (epochs 143–198 vs 196–199), consistent with a stronger feature space.

2 · Setup & notes

  • Encoders — 260624_channelvit_{04m,4m}_plinder_otf_s0_pretrain, e99 EMA weights. Channel-ViT (patch_embed_mode=channel_group, groups [7,4,1,1], patch 8, 13 input channels = 7 lig + 4 poc + density + ‖∇ρ‖), pretrained on PLINDER OTF with the C+D+G density-MAE objective. 0.4M = dim 64 × depth 4 (1,191,808 params); 4M = dim 192 × depth 6 (5,628,416 params). The "0.4M/4M" tags are config-tier nicknames; the figures above are the measured frozen-encoder param counts.
  • Probe — scripts/04_probe.sh --condition atomblob_density_gradmag --atom_source ligvdw --voxel v5 --split lp_edrscc --seeds 3. Features extracted once per encoder over the 6,451-complex local pool (0 errors), then a frozen 2-layer MLP regresses pK. The encoder is LayerNorm-only + mean-pooled, so batching is result-neutral.
  • Split — frozen, hash-verified voxbind/splits/lp_edrscc_v2.csv: LP-PDBBind new_split ∩ ED-available ∩ (lig & poc RSCC ≥ 0.8), non-covalent, Kd/Ki-only. Label = pK (−log Kd/Ki). Novel-target (sequence-clustered) partition.
  • Takeaway — at this pretraining budget, encoder capacity is not yet saturated: the jump from 1.19M → 5.63M buys a real, seed-stable gain in affinity transfer (and beats the supervised baselines). Next: probe an even larger tier and/or more pretraining to find where the frozen-probe curve plateaus.
