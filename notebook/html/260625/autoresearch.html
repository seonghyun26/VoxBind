Reading it

Density lifts ChannelViT +0.033 over coords (best 0.637 vs coords-ChannelViT 0.604). The winning structure is group density+gradmag together, keep lig/poc separate ([7,4,2] > [11,2] > [7,4,1,1] > [11,1,1]). Every knob tried so far hurts — raising mask ratio (0.6/0.75) or recon weight (0.3 tied, 1.0 worse) only loses ground. Open-ended until stopped.

Reference: TargetDiff EGNN (supervised) scores ρ 0.536 on its own LP-PDBBind split — a different, larger test set, not directly comparable.
