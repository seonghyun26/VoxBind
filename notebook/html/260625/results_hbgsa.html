HBGSA affinity baseline — LP-EDRSCC v2

📅 2026-06-24 🧪 PDBBind v2020 · lp_edrscc_v2 📊 test n=1,320 · 3 seeds · mean ± std ⚙ voxbind + hbgsa_pymol env · RTX PRO 6000 (GPU 3)
TL;DR. HBGSA is an external, fully-supervised reference (4 branches: H-bond graph + protein-seq + pocket + SMILES) — not a frozen probe. On the Kd/Ki-only, RSCC-filtered lp_edrscc_v2 split the paper-faithful 3.06M model clearly beats the 40M scale-up: test Pearson 0.565 vs 0.425, RMSE 1.568 vs 2.247, R² 0.214 vs −0.649. The 40M still ranks above chance (ρ 0.42) but its regression is mis-calibrated — negative R² (worse than predicting the mean) and seed-unstable RMSE (±0.34) — a textbook over-parameterization symptom on only 3,850 training complexes. Bigger ≠ better here. Both rows are final (3 seeds, full early-stopped training).

3 · Setup & notes

  • Model — from-scratch reimplementation of HBGSA (Hydrogen-Bond Graph + Self-Attention, arXiv 2604.23115). Four branches, each → embedding, concatenated → FC head: H-bond graph (PyMOL-extracted protein–ligand H-bonds as nodes, dynamic-KNN, 2× GCNConv), protein sequence & SMILES (multi-scale dilated-conv residual tower + 1D self-attention), and binding pocket (1D conv over {pdb}_pocket.pdb residues).
  • 3.06M — paper-faithful default dims (emb/d_model 128, conv_channels 213; 3,064,907 params). 40M — width-scaled (emb/d_model 512, gcn/pocket/head 512, conv_channels 736; 39.6M params). hbgsa_baseline/run_edrscc_v2.sh.
  • Training — fully supervised on pK, end-to-end. Hybrid loss SmoothL1 + 50·(1−Pearson), AdamW lr 1e-3, batch 64, ≤150 epochs, early-stop on val Spearman (patience 20), target standardized on train (de-standardized before metrics → RMSE/MAE in pK units). 3 seeds {0,1,2}.
  • Pipeline — labels from raw/LP_PDBBind.csv (pK = value), membership + split from the frozen lp_edrscc_v2.csv. Caches built once (5,987 each): H-bond graphs via PyMOL in a minimal hbgsa_pymol conda env; pocket-seqs via Biopython. Training runs in the voxbind env (torch 2.10+cu128) — the only env whose torch supports these Blackwell sm_120 GPUs.
  • Takeaway — the affinity signal here is bounded by the 3,850-complex training set; the paper-size 3.06M model is the right capacity, and naively scaling to 40M hurts (mis-calibration, instability) without small-data regularization (lower LR + warmup, stronger weight-decay/dropout, longer schedule). A tuned 40M sweep is the obvious next step if the scale-up is worth pursuing.
