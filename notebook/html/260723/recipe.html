The winning recipe — cgd0.2 frozen-encoder VoxBind

A density-conditioned VoxBind that generates ligands without any ligand-density hint (apo), conditioned on a frozen density encoder pretrained with channel-group dropout (HCS, p=0.2). The dropout makes ligand-free input in-distribution and yields a better binding-affinity representation — the honest, leakage-free model that is already ahead on the hard pockets.

TL;DR

Pretrain the density encoder with channel-group dropout 0.2 → freeze it → condition VoxBind on it with a ControlNet-style zero-init branch, generating in the apo (ligand-density-masked) setting. The encoder gets +0.025 binding-affinity ρ (0.617 → 0.642) over the standard pretrain, and the apo generator is already the best model on the hardest pockets at 27% of training, with the best pose quality of any run.

1 The recipe

Two stages. First pretrain the encoder; then train the generator conditioned on it. Everything is the same as the previous frozen-enc setup except the two boxed changes.

Stage 1 · encoder

cgd0.2 density-MAE pretrain

  • atomblob7 v2.1 ChannelViT density-MAE, ~45.7M params
  • 13-ch input, 4 groups: ligand(7) · pocket(4) · density(1) · gradmag(1)
  • patch-MAE mask 0.5, PLINDER v2.1, 100 epochs
  • ★ channel_group_dropout = 0.2 (HCS): each whole group — including the ligand — is token-dropped ~20% of forwards
# exp 260718 · checkpoint_e0099
torchrun --nproc_per_node=4 train_density.py \
  --config-name=..._channelvit_mae_40m_plinder_v2p1_box \
  model.channel_group_dropout=0.2
Stage 2 · generator

apo frozen-enc VoxBind (σ0.9)

  • keep the trainable ligand + pocket encoders (vanilla VoxBind)
  • add the frozen cgd0.2 encoder via a zero-init density_proj (ControlNet zero-conv)
  • ★ apo: density_mask_ligand=true — blank density+gradmag inside the ligand footprint, so no co-crystal ligand density leaks
  • walk-jump, σ = 0.9, 350 epochs
# fuse: x = lig_enc(lig) + poc_enc(poc)
#         + density_proj([x, enc([lig=0,poc,dens,gradmag])])
torchrun --nproc_per_node=4 train_ddp.py \
  --config-name=..._frozenenc_..._v2p1_ligmask \
  smooth_sigma=0.9 \
  model.density_pretrained_path=<cgd0.2 ckpt>

The zero-init fusion means training starts exactly as vanilla VoxBind and learns to fold in density — simple, stable, and it keeps the pocket CNN that does the spatial generalizing.

3 What we learned (design rationale)

Channel-group dropout in pretraining KEEP

Dropping whole groups (incl. the ligand) ~20% of forwards makes ligand-free (apo) input in-distribution for the frozen encoder — dissolving the leakage-vs-OOD confound — and, as a bonus, improves the binding-affinity representation (+0.025 ρ).

ControlNet-style late fusion, keep both encoders KEEP

Zero-init density_proj added on top of the trainable ligand + pocket encoders. Simple, stable, and it preserves the pocket CNN's spatial inductive bias — the thing that generalizes.

Apo (no ligand density) is the honest model KEEP

Frozen-holo's −8.24 dock edge is partly informational leakage: the co-crystal density encodes where the ligand sits. It lives on easy pockets and evaporates on hard ones. Masking the ligand density (apo) removes the leak; the poses stay physical, and generalization improves.

v3 fusion — replacing the pocket encoder DROP

Deleting the pocket CNN and routing everything through the frozen ViT overfit: train/val gap flipped from −17% to +31%, and it produced the best raw Vina Score (−6.01) but the worst Vina Dock (−7.16) — deceptively-placed molecules that fall apart on redock. Negative result.

Status & caveats. The encoder result (ρ 0.642) is definitive. The generation evidence is early: the hard-pocket lead is a probe at ep94/350 with 10 mols/pocket, and the pose-quality row is the apo setting on the previous encoder. The apo-cgd0.2 generator is paused at ep130 (checkpoint saved); the full 79-pocket ep350 eval is the number to confirm the win.
