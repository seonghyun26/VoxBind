1Macrocycles

Do the ligands VoxBind generates contain macrocycles, how does their rate compare to the training/reference corpora (CrossDocked, PLINDER), and does being a macrocycle correlate with a better Vina docking score? Raw correlation. On the generated set, macrocycles (10 of 618) dock better by Δ = -2.66 kcal/mol (mean -9.98 vs -7.32), point-biserial r = -0.158, Mann–Whitney p = 0.0001 → strongly significant. But read §3 before believing it — macrocycles are large by construction and Vina scores track molecular size, so this is almost certainly a size artefact.

1cIs it real, or just molecular size?

A macrocycle contributes 12–23 ring atoms on its own, so macrocyclic ligands are necessarily heavy — and Vina scores grow more negative with heavy-atom count regardless of binding. Before attributing the §2 gap to macrocyclicity we control for size two ways: (a) an OLS with n_atoms + macrocycle flag as predictors, and (b) a size-matched comparison against only the large non-macrocycles.

Verdict. Docking is dominated by size (Pearson corr(dock, atoms) = -0.656; macrocycles average 36 vs 22 heavy atoms). After adjusting for atom count the macrocycle-specific effect is -0.41 ± 0.52 kcal/mol (p = 0.437, not significant), and against size-matched large non-macrocycles the gap is Δ = -0.87 kcal/mol (p = 0.153, not significant). → The apparent macrocycle→better-dock correlation is largely a molecular-size effect, not a macrocycle-specific one.
Notes & caveats.
  • Definition. Macrocycle = largest SSSR ring ≥ 12 atoms (IUPAC-style). SSSR can split a bridged macrocycle into smaller rings, so this is a mild under-count; the ≥ 8 column bounds it above.
  • Docking sign. Vina scores are negative; "lower = better". A negative Δ / point-biserial r means macrocycles bind more favourably.
  • Power. Only 10 generated macrocycles, so every docking test on them is low-powered; the size confound (§3) is the dominant caveat, mirroring the size-confound seen in the DSMBind baseline.
  • Provenance. Generated set = voxbind/exps/260518_voxbind_10k_noise/samples/res_ep99_test; CrossDocked = voxbind/dataset/data/crossdocked_pocket10 (unique ligands); PLINDER = voxbind/splits/plinder/v2/plinder_selected.csv.
