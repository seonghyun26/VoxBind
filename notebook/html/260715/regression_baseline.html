Structure-based affinity regression — CheapNet & BindNet

Two new supervised baselines trained on our frozen lp_edrscc_v2 split (LP-PDBBind ∩ ED ∩ RSCC ≥ 0.8, Kd/Ki; 3850 / 817 / 1320), reported against the existing baseline suite and the VoxBind density probe. Metrics: Pearson r, Spearman ρ, RMSE on the held-out test set, mean ± std over 3 seeds (best-validation checkpoint).

1 CheapNet & BindNet vs. the baseline suite — lp_edrscc_v2

CheapNet (ICLR 2025) — cross-attention over hierarchical (atom → cluster) protein/ligand representations on a GIGN message-passing backbone; trained from scratch. BindNet (ICLR 2024) — a Uni-Mol complex cross-net; its large pretrained checkpoint is access-restricted on Google Drive, so per the brief we train the architecture from scratch on our split (a lower bound on its pretrained potential). Rows below the divider are prior baselines and the density probe, for reference.

3 Method & reproducibility

  • CheapNet — base/cheapnet/. GIGN-style graphs (5 Å PyMOL pocket, 35-dim atom features, intra + inter ≤5 Å edges) bucketed by the frozen split; 3 HIL blocks → DiffPool clustering ([28,157]) → ligand/pocket cross-attention. Config = authors' cross_dataset (batch 128, ≤800 epochs, early-stop 100, Adam lr 5e-4). 5959/5987 complexes built.
  • BindNet — base/bindnet/. Uni-Mol/unicore affinity_regres (complex_crnet, recycling 1, seperate-CLS); ATOM3D-LBA .npy data (256-atom pocket + ligand, atomic numbers, pK). Trained from scratch on GPU (batch 16, lr 1e-4, early-stop on valid_pearson). Pretrained checkpoint was access-restricted (gdown denied) — to finetune the pretrained model, obtain checkpoint_bindnet.pt and pass CKPT=… bash _edrscc/train_bindnet.sh.
  • Both use the same LP_PDBBind pK labels and structures as the GET / ProFSA baselines, for comparability. Repro scripts + READMEs are under each baseline's folder.
