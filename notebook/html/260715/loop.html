VoxBind · PLINDER v2 · autoresearch loop

Bigger data + bigger model: does the recipe change?

A brief self-paced autoresearch loop on a balanced ~100M encoder (dim 640 × depth 18 × 10 heads, 99.47M) pretrained on PLINDER v2 (112K, C+D+G, ChannelViT [7,4,2]), sweeping the recipe the v1 campaign had frozen. Frozen-probe affinity on lp_edrscc_v2 (Kd/Ki, 3850/817/1320, 3 seeds). Sweep runs are 50 ep / probe e49 for speed — relative comparisons only; the winner gets full-trained to 100 ep for the headline table. Built 2026-07-06.

Why this loop

The v1 campaign (~30 trials) plateaued at ρ 0.637 and nothing beat it — capacity, depth, masking strategy, pretext, finetune all tied-or-hurt. Then the 2×2 size×corpus grid showed more data + more params both regressed the frozen probe. Open question: were those recipes (mask 0.50, etc.) tuned for the small v1 regime? This loop re-opens the recipe on the bigger model + bigger corpus.

Round 1 (mask sweep) — the plateau broke. mask 0.75 (ρ 0.644) beats the campaign-default 0.50 (0.614) by +0.030, and at only 50 ep it already clears the 0.637 that held across the entire v1 campaign. Bigger data+model wants much heavier masking (MAE-style) — the first real lever after ~30 trials of ties.

Round 2 — the recipe converged. Two clean results: (1) mask peaks at 0.75 — pushing to 0.85 (T3, 0.623) overshoots, so the curve is 0.614 → 0.644 → 0.623. (2) More heads hurt — h16 (T4, 0.633, head_dim 40) < h10 (0.644, head_dim 64); the bigger head_dim wins. The leader stays T2: d640/L18/h10 @ mask 0.75 = 0.644.

Round 3. T5 (depth reshape, dim 512×28, h8/head_dim 64) = ρ 0.616 — going even deeper overshoots (−0.028 vs the balanced d640/L18). So the recipe has a clean optimum on every axis: mask 0.75 (0.85 too high), d640×18 balanced (d768×12 too wide, d512×28 too deep), head_dim 64 (h16 too many heads). Winner = d640/L18/h10 @ mask 0.75 = 0.644.

T6 = the full run (100ep) — training OVERSHOOTS too. Continuing the 0.644 model from e49 to e99 drops it to ρ 0.618 (−0.026). Same trajectory, so it's clean: the frozen-probe quality peaks at ~e49 and degrades by e99 — the encoder overfits the reconstruction task. So the epoch budget is another axis with an early optimum (like mask 0.75 and the balanced arch). Consequence: at matched 100 ep this config = 0.618 < the 40M·v1 grid ceiling (0.637); the 0.644 break is a 50-ep number. The grid (all 100 ep, mask 0.50) may be both under-masked and over-trained — see the overnight hypotheses.
