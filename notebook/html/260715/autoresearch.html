VoxBind · PLINDER v2 · recipe autoresearch

Re-tuning the recipe for a bigger model + corpus

A single-thread autoresearch loop: a ~100M ChannelViT [7,4,2] C+D+G encoder on PLINDER v2 (112K), sweeping the recipe the v1 campaign had frozen. Frozen-probe affinity on lp_edrscc_v2 (Kd/Ki, 3 seeds). Trials are 50 ep / probe e49 unless noted; the grey G- points are the pre-campaign 100 ep / mask 0.50 baseline. Noise floor: pretrain-seed spread ≈ ±0.02 — only effects that clear it are real.

Dramatic differences & noticeable points

G4 · width-98M · v2 (ρ 0.595). 'Capacity/width HURTS' — the low point. Later REFRAMED (see T13).
T2 · mask 0.75 (ρ 0.644). ★ THE lever: +0.030 — first thing to clear the 0.637 ceiling. (Seed-lucky, see T14.)
T13 · width-98M @0.75 (ρ 0.627). ★ REFRAME: +0.032 vs its mask-0.50 grid (0.595). 'Width regressed' was under-masking.
G1 · 40M · v1 (ρ 0.637). Prior ceiling — unbeaten across the ~30-trial v1 campaign.
T6 · 100 epochs (ρ 0.618). Training OVERSHOOTS — same run 0.644@50ep → 0.618@100ep (over-specialization).
T12 · 40M @0.75 (ρ 0.623). Heavy mask does NOT help the small model (tied) → mask×capacity interaction.
S1 · 150M @0.75 (ρ 0.626). Scaling does NOT re-open — capacity still peaks ~100M even at the right mask.
T14 · epoch re-run (ρ 0.633). Re-run of T2 peaks 0.633, not 0.644 → pretrain-seed noise ±0.02. 0.644 was a favorable draw.
C · 40M @0.50·50ep (ρ 0.622). Tied with 40M@0.75 (0.6225) → at matched 50ep the mask ratio is FLAT for the small model. Interaction airtight.
SP · splat head (iso) (ρ 0.628). Physical-prior recon (density = sum of atom-Gaussians, not free voxels) = TIED with patch-MLP baseline. First alt-objective that doesn't regress (vs denoise −0.05, contrastive −0.14).
HCS · HCS chan-drop (ρ 0.635). Channel-group dropout (drops whole groups incl the 7-ch ligand group) = TIED with baseline 0.631±.010 (within noise, no accuracy cost). ADOPT for free robustness to a zero/missing ligand channel → ligand-masked generation & pocket-only inputs.
B4 · block 4 (1Å) (ρ 0.604). Finer masking HURTS (−0.03, clears noise) — 1Å holes too easy to inpaint. block8 (2Å) stays optimal; geometry didn't shift with mask0.75.
Multi-seed confirmation (3 pretrain seeds each). The single-draw points above are noisy; the real numbers with error bars: 100M 0.631±0.010 · 150M 0.623±0.003 · width-98M 0.621±0.004 — so 100M is marginally best and capacity past it doesn't help (150M≈width-98M). Splat recon objective: iso 0.628 / aniso 0.620 — both TIED-or-below the patch-MLP baseline (0.631), anisotropy doesn't help → physical splat is a wash for the encoder.
Bottom line. Re-tuning the mask ratio (0.50→0.75) is the one robust win (+0.030, confirmed independently on width-98M at +0.032) and it reversed the campaign's "scaling hurts" verdicts — at the right mask, the ~100M models beat the 40M. The capacity sweet spot shifts up (~40M→~100M) but still peaks. Everything else — exact best number, epoch-peak, fine arch, alt-objectives (splat/aniso) — sits inside the ±0.01–0.02 seed noise. The headline recipe lands 0.631 ± 0.010 (3 seeds), straddling the old 0.637 ceiling rather than clearing it.
