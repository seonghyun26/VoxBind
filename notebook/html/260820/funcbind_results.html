The shortfall is not uniform: the fine-tune matches vanilla exactly on two targets and beats it on 1w0e, then collapses to single digits on four others. 3dv5 yields nothing from either model, so it is excluded from all downstream averages — 9 targets, not 10.

Caveat — this ran at n_attempts=1. The paper configuration retries up to 10 times per target, so a single failed attempt here is not recoverable and the absolute yields are lower than a paper-setting run would give. Both models had the identical budget, so the comparison is fair, but read these as relative numbers. Targets with 2–3 molecules also make per-target statistics very noisy for the fine-tune.

28 of 213 (vanilla) and 21 of 118 (fine-tuned) molecules scored so far, across 5 targets each; 4 of 9 reference ligands docked. High affinity is deliberately not reported yet — it needs every molecule of a target plus that target's reference, and no target is complete.

Ignore the Score and Min means; read the medians. The means are positive (+7.8 and +43.5) because a generated macrocycle that overlaps the receptor in its as-generated pose scores in the hundreds, and one such pose swamps an average over 20–30 molecules. The medians (−7.37 / −7.46) are the honest summary. Dock mode re-searches the pose, which is why its mean and median agree and why it is the only one of the three that is stable at this sample size.

On the molecules scored so far the two models are within noise of each other on Dock (−10.31 vs −10.04), and both beat the reference cyclic peptides (−9.69). Treat this as directional only — 21–28 molecules over 5 targets is far too few to separate them.

Every molecule from both models is a single connected fragment and cyclizes — the structural contract of MCP generation holds on both sides. Diversity, QED and SA are indistinguishable. The one real difference is size: the fine-tune's peptides average 7.3 more heavy atoms (55.1 vs 47.8).

QED ≈ 0.2 and SA ≈ 0.48 are expected here, not a red flag. Both are calibrated on small-molecule drugs; a 48–55 heavy-atom macrocyclic peptide is far outside their design range and scores low by construction. They are useful for comparing the two models against each other and meaningless as absolute drug-likeness claims. Note also that larger ligands tend to dock better simply by burying more surface, so the fine-tune's size advantage is a confound for any Vina comparison — worth keeping in mind when the full docking numbers land.

6Reproducing

Sampling — four chunks, one per GPU, two per model

NAME=cmp10/vanilla_a GPU=0 IDS=[0,1,2,3,4] NTARGETS=5 NPR=25 NATT=1 BSRC=32 NCHAINS=256 bash scripts/run_mcpp_sampling.sh

CONFIG=sample_fb_mcpp_holo_density FB_PATH=exps/funcbind/20260816_..._ga32_r3 <same>

Docking — reuses the VoxBind eval driver over an assembled eval tree

python voxbind/exps/frozenenc_probes/run_docking_eval_parallel.py <eval_root> --workers 5 --cpu 2 --exh 16

CONFIG and FB_PATH were added to run_mcpp_sampling.sh for this comparison — the script previously hardcoded the vanilla checkpoint and config. The fine-tune needs both overrides: its architecture comes from the checkpoint's own config, the holo-density dataset wiring from the sampling config.
