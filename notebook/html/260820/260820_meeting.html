1.2Similarity

Main metrics summarize 2D structure, scaffold identity, and 3D shape. Sub metrics check fingerprint sensitivity. Similarity cells read mean / max.

  • Morgan: ECFP radius 2, 2,048-bit fingerprint; Tanimoto similarity.
  • MACCS: 167 structural keys; Tanimoto similarity.
  • AtomPair: hashed 2,048-bit atom-pair fingerprint; Tanimoto similarity.
  • RDKit: topological path fingerprint; Tanimoto similarity.
  • Dice: count-based Morgan radius 2 fingerprint; Dice similarity.
  • Scaffold match: exact canonical Bemis–Murcko scaffold match rate.
  • 3D shape: RDKit shape Tanimoto in the shared pocket frame, without alignment.
  • Aggregation: compute mean and maximum against the reference within each pocket, then macro-average across pockets.
