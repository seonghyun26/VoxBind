1MCP design results

Mid-training check on the receptor-ED density fine-tune of the macrocyclic-peptide generator, sampled at 26.1M fine-tuning samples and scored against the same run at 8.21M (the 08-27 meeting) and 3.17M (08-20), plus the density-free vanilla FuncBind. Same 10 held-out targets and the same sampling budget every round, so all four arms are directly comparable. measured

Bottom line The 8.21M regression undid itself; the binding gap did not move. At 8.21M the model had collapsed to small peptides (median 35 heavy atoms) and half the yield. At 26.1M both have largely recovered — 186 molecules against vanilla's 213, median size back to 41 — but Vina Dock sits at −8.33, statistically the same as 8.21M's −8.38 and still about 1 kcal/mol behind vanilla's −9.34. Three checkpoints spanning 8× in fine-tuning have moved yield and size around without once closing that gap. Density conditioning is not buying binding affinity in this model.

1.1Setup

  • Base model: FuncBind MCP fb_unified, density-free, 87.2M samples seen — the “vanilla” arm, unchanged since 08-20 and never re-sampled (same checkpoint, settings and seed, so its 08-20 molecules remain the baseline).
  • Fine-tune: receptor-ED ControlNet with zero-init zero-convs on that base, encoder trunk frozen. Three checkpoints of one resume chain, measured in acc_iter (fine-tuning samples seen, base model's 87.2M not included): _r3 3.17M, _r10 8.21M, _r14 26,112,876. Never rank these by epoch — every resume restarts the epoch counter, so _r14 reads epoch 24 while being the furthest along of 15 runs.
  • Targets: 10 held-out MCP test targets — 1bm2 · 1bxo · 1jd2 · 1kmh · 1w0e · 2bdx · 2gpl · 3dv5 · 3e7a · 3egh.
  • Sampling: 25 samples per receptor, 256 chains, 1 attempt, seed 1 — identical every round.
  • Docking: whole deposited receptor (<pdb>-protein.pdb), box from the crystal cyclic peptide (<pdb>-CP.sdf), Vina exhaustiveness 16.

1.2Yield

Molecules returned per target out of a 25-molecule quota, on an identical budget. Vanilla is best in every bin, by 0.4 to 1.4 kcal/mol, in all three rounds. The three fine-tune checkpoints sit on top of each other: 26.1M is the best of them at 40–49 and 50–59 and the worst at 20–29, which is the pattern of noise, not of progress. What 26.1M did buy is coverage — it is the only fine-tune arm with enough molecules to be readable in all five bins.

1.5Caveats and what to do next

  • One attempt, one seed. n_attempts=1 against the paper configuration's 10, so absolute yields are low for every arm. The budget is identical across arms, so the comparison holds, but the yields are relative numbers.
  • Mid-training, not converged. All three fine-tune points are snapshots of one live run, taken by pausing training; the run continues from _r15.
  • The 8.21M reading was partly transient. Its size collapse and yield loss both reverted, so a single checkpoint should not be read as a trend — which is the argument for checking at this cadence.
  • Next: the binding gap is the one thing that has not moved in three rounds and 8× of fine-tuning. Worth deciding whether to keep training this recipe at all, versus changing what the density conditioning feeds, before another 20M samples go by.
